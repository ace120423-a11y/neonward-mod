NeonWard original synthesized gun foley.
Generated deterministically by tools/build_gun_audio.py using oscillators and seeded noise.
No third-party recordings or samples are used.
14 mono Vorbis assets at 32 kHz; sound distance is configured in sounds.json.
cryo.ogg is a sustained loop, stopped by the client on trigger release, weapon change,
death, pause, world change or disconnect. charge.ogg is a railgun charge loop.
